Privacy Policy for Callzuno Effective Date: October 24, 2024 Last Updated: March 2025 Callzuno ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application (Callzuno: Caller ID & Shield). 1. Information We Collect Location Data: When you explicitly enable the Family Protection feature, we collect precise real-time location data (GPS) to allow authorized family circle members to view your live location and receive SOS emergency alerts even when the app is running in the background. Call Logs & Contacts: With your explicit runtime permission, Callzuno accesses device call logs and contacts to provide core Caller ID identification, spam detection, call screening, custom ringtones, and family contact lookup. Phone Number & Account Info: We collect your phone number during sign-up for user authentication and account management via secure backend services. Device & Ad Identifiers: We utilize Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google Play Services, which collect anonymous device identifiers, IP addresses, and diagnostic logs for ad serving, analytics, and fraud prevention. 2. How We Use Your Information To deliver real-time Caller ID matching, phone number identification, and spam call blocking. To display live GPS coordinates to approved members within your private Family Protection circle during active sessions or SOS emergencies. To sync subscription entitlements via Google Play Billing. To maintain application security, performance, and compliance. 3. Zero Voice Call Recording Guarantee Callzuno strictly DOES NOT record, listen to, store, or transmit any audio voice calls or conversations. Your phone calls remain 100% private and untouched on your device. 4. Data Sharing & Security We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. All network communications between Callzuno and server infrastructure are encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS / SSL protocols. 5. Your Choices, Rights & Account Deletion You can revoke permissions (Location, Contacts, Call Logs) at any time through your device settings. You can also request complete account and data deletion directly inside the app under Settings > Account > Delete Account or by visiting our online account deletion request page or emailing our support team.
